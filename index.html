BBDBuyEU finds by category

Use a product type and one detail to narrow the search. For low-top sneakers, try the colour or upper material. For a denim jacket, start with the cut and wash. Save the original seller link, item ID, selected colourway and size so you can find the same listing again.

Compare garment measurements with a piece you already wear. Check chest width and sleeve length for hoodies, shoulder width and body length for T-shirts, and waist, rise and inseam for trousers. For matching sets, check whether the top and bottom can be sized separately.

BBDBuyEU QC photo checklist

Quality-control (QC) photos help you inspect the selected variant. For shoes, ask for side, heel and outsole views, plus an insole measurement if fit is uncertain. For a jersey, check seam alignment, lettering placement and the fabric close-up.

For headwear, check crown depth and the adjustment range. For a scarf, confirm the dimensions and edge finish. For jewellery, look for clasp details and ask the seller to confirm the material. Ask whether the images show your specific item or are reference photos.

BBDBuyEU shipping to Europe

Build a delivered-cost estimate in your own currency. Record the item subtotal, local delivery to the warehouse, service charges, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Ask which costs are already included in the quote.

Compare shipping quotes for the same postcode, packed dimensions and parcel weight. Ask how a shoe box or padded jacket affects the billable weight. Check the seller’s return window and whether you can request a return after warehouse inspection.

Frequently asked questions

What is a BBDBuyEU spreadsheet?

It is a way to organise product links by category. This independent guide explains what to compare and sends readers to HipoBuyFind for category browsing.

Can QC photos prove a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can show visible condition, construction and measurements. For branded purchases, check seller provenance and use a trusted authentication service when needed.

How do I estimate delivery costs in Europe?

Get a quote for your country and postcode using the packed dimensions and estimated weight. Check which fees the quote includes, then verify applicable taxes or duties with your destination’s customs authority.

Are the pictured items available through the category links?

The photos are editorial references. Each category link opens a HipoBuyFind category where you can check individual listings for availability, sizes and seller information.
